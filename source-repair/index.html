02 / WHY THE NEGATIVE CONTROLS MATTER
Partial repairs are not enough.
Adding an explanation can preserve a warning without preventing a tool call. Changing the stop reason still leaves an executable call. Suppressing the call may still erase why it was blocked. The successful reference changes the two affected provider functions together, while leaving the shared collector unmodified.
